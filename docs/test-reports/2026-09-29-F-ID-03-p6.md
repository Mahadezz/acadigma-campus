## 1. Scope

**What this Part is.** On `/app/staff/team`, an owner or admin now taps "Manage" on an active member and, in one sheet, changes their role (with a plain-language preview of what they gain or lose), assigns a custom label, and edits their staff details (employee code — generated when left blank — department, work phone). A new `/app/settings/labels` screen creates, edits and deletes the school's custom labels ("Vice-Principal" over a base role). A label changes the title shown next to a name, never what the person can do. Decisions: D-111.

**Acceptance criteria covered** (spec §9 — Part 6 is §4.5/§4.8, whose relevant checks are AC-3, AC-4, AC-5, AC-14):

| #     | Criterion                                                  | Covered by                                                                                                                      |
| ----- | ---------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| AC-3  | A member cannot change their own role                      | `39b_member_staff_fields.sql` (guard); `members-part6.integration.test.ts` "refuses to change your own role"                    |
| AC-4  | An admin changing/removing an owner gets refused           | `39b_` "an admin cannot promote anyone to owner"; repo pre-check `FORBIDDEN_OWNER_TARGET`                                       |
| AC-5  | Last owner cannot be demoted                               | guard `23514 → LAST_OWNER_BLOCKED` (mapped in `members.ts`; guard proven in `03_`/`39a_`)                                       |
| AC-14 | A label over a base role changes the title but not `can()` | `39b_` "assigning a label sets label_id and leaves the role unchanged"; `roleChange.test.ts` (permissions derived from `can()`) |

**Out of scope for this Part** — and where it is handled instead:

- Removal, leaving, ownership transfer — Part 7.
- Teachers/staff editing their **own** staff fields — a separate self-service surface (this screen is owner/admin).
- `work_email`, per-subject `subject_ids`, label `name_bn` — no columns; OQ-7/8/9, `docs/plan/OWNER-QUESTIONS.md`.
- `member.role_changed` / `.staff_fields_updated` / `.label_assigned` notifications — F-ID-07 (no fan-out yet); the generic `workspace_members.update` audit row records the actor (D-51).

**Risk areas:** the definer `update_member_staff_fields` (owner/admin gate is the boundary, plus code generation and the unique-code clash) — pgTAP escalation + generation + `EMPLOYEE_NO_TAKEN`; that a label can never change permissions — consequences derived from `can()`; that the dropdown can never mint an owner/parent — the input enum.
