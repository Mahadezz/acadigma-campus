## 1. Scope

A person can delete their own account from `/account/security` (Settings → Your account, the account page link in the personal/family shells): two-step sheet, type `DELETE`, password. Nothing is removed for 30 days; a banner with **Keep my account** shows on every shell. A nightly pg_cron job then purges the account: personal workspace and user-level rows removed, profile turned into a "Deleted user" tombstone, school memberships `removed`, every school record kept, `audit_events` untouched. Blocked while the person is the only active owner of any school.
