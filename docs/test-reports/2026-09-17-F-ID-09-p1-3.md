## 1. Scope

**What this Part is.** A workspace owner can open `/app/audit`, filter their workspace's audit trail (date preset, category, severity, free text), tap a row to see a plain-language sentence with a before/after diff, and follow "Show everything from this action" to every row sharing one `correlation_id`. Underneath, every tenant table now writes to an append-only `audit_events` table through exactly two writers (`app.tg_audit()` / `app.log_audit_event()`), against a shared, CI-checked action catalogue, with severity, actor kind, changed-field diffing, a universal secret deny-list, and correlation-id threading via a PostgREST pre-request hook.

**Out of scope for this Part** (Part 4, or a documented cut):

- Export (`exportAuditEvents`), `/platform/audit`, `platform.audit_viewed`, the weekly integrity job — all Part 4.
- Full-text search over the rendered sentence (needs a stored/indexed sentence column) — filter `q` matches the raw `action` string only.
- Custom date-range picker — presets only (today/7d/30d/all).
- `countAuditEvents`, `GET /api/files/[id]` for exports — not needed without export.
- `targetExists` on the detail DTO ("this record no longer exists") — would need a per-table existence lookup; not built.

**Risk areas** — where testing effort went:

- The append-only + read-policy boundary (RLS), because that is the entire point of the feature (Base44 finding 5).
- The catalogue-enforcement contract (`app.log_audit_event` raising on an unknown action) and its CI parity check, because a silent drift here is exactly the failure mode this feature exists to close.
- Redaction: the universal secret deny-list and the new free-text-nulling mechanism, proven against a throwaway pgTAP scratch table since no shipped table needs it yet.
- Bundle size: `react-hook-form` + Radix `Select` pulled `/app/audit` over the 250 kB budget on the first pass; fixed with `next/dynamic` code-splitting for the filter and detail sheets (see §6).

## 10. Review round 1 — fixes applied on `feat/identity-audit`

Senior review of PR #6 (triggers / RLS / append-only, D-37) found six blocking
defects. All are fixed on this branch; the numbers above predate the fixes,
except the Vitest totals, which are re-run (236 passed, 16 files).

| #   | Finding                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Fix                                                                                                                                                                          |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| B-1 | The universal deny-list was applied to `changed_fields` only, so an UPDATE that touched one ordinary column wrote every UNCHANGED secret (a push token, an invitation token) into `before`/`after` verbatim                                                                     | deny-list now runs over the whole payload key set; pgTAP regression added                                                                                                    |
| B-2 | The `app`-schema grants sweep handed `authenticated` EXECUTE on `app.purge_expired_audit_events` (floor: 1 year) and on the `attach_*` DDL helpers — any signed-in user could delete six years of every tenant's trail, or silently re-configure the audit trigger on any table | sweep now excludes the five privileged functions; purge floor raised to 7 years and it refuses an application `role` GUC                                                     |
| B-3 | Contact data (`workspace_invitations.email`/`.phone`, `workspace_members.phone`, `school_profiles.contact_*`) reached the trail raw — §5.3 says masked at write time, acceptance criterion 10 says `r***@gmail.com`                                                             | `app.audit_contact_pattern()` + `app.mask_email` / `app.mask_phone` applied in the trigger, mirrored in `redact.ts` and re-applied in `buildDiffRows` for pre-migration rows |
| B-4 | Health, medical and religion columns had no universal rule at all, despite §5.3 and COMPLIANCE-PDPA §4.1 marking them sensitive; NID and birth-certificate numbers were not on the secret list either                                                                           | `app.audit_sensitive_pattern()` (value nulled, name kept) and the ID-document terms added to `app.audit_secret_pattern()`                                                    |
| B-5 | `grant select on public.audit_events to authenticated` (0003) left the deprecated raw `ip`/`user_agent` readable on the base table, so the view's omission of them was a convention                                                                                             | replaced with a column-level grant matching the view's column list                                                                                                           |
| B-6 | `08_audit_substrate.sql` asserted nothing about append-only, the purge, or a malformed correlation header                                                                                                                                                                       | §11–13 added: 26 → 56 assertions                                                                                                                                             |
