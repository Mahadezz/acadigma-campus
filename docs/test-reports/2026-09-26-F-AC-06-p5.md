## 1. Scope

An owner locks marks, presses "Compute results" on the exam page and sees "Results computed: n students, p passed, f failed"; "View results" lists one section in rank order (rank, "tied", name, roll, GPA, grade, pass/fail) and each row opens to its per-subject marks, %, letter and grade point. Results are computed in SQL from the exam's grading snapshot and held to a golden fixture that the TypeScript reference must match too.

## 3. The golden fixture

Class 6 A, twelve students × six papers (P1–P5 out of 100, pass 33; P6 out of 50, pass 16.50), BD scale, F zeroes the GPA. Hand-computed:

| Student | Marks (P1–P6)                  | Total        | %     | GPA  | Grade | Result     | Rank |
| ------- | ------------------------------ | ------------ | ----- | ---- | ----- | ---------- | ---- |
| S01     | 85, 90, 80, 95, 88, 45         | 483 / 550    | 87.82 | 5.00 | A+    | pass       | 1    |
| S02     | 75, 72, 80, 85, 90, 40         | 442 / 550    | 80.36 | 4.67 | A     | pass       | 2    |
| S03     | 70, 80, 75, 85, 90, 40         | 440 / 550    | 80.00 | 4.67 | A     | pass       | 3    |
| S04     | 80, 70, 85, 75, 90, 40         | 440 / 550    | 80.00 | 4.67 | A     | pass       | 3    |
| S12     | 75, 72, exempt, 65, 55, exempt | 267 / 400    | 66.75 | 3.63 | A-    | pass       | 5    |
| S05     | 79.5, 60, 60, 60, 60, 30       | 349.50 / 550 | 63.55 | 3.58 | A-    | pass       | 6    |
| S06     | 65, 65, 65, 65, exempt, 25     | 285 / 450    | 63.33 | 3.40 | B     | pass       | 7    |
| S09     | 33, 33, 33, 33, 33, 16.5       | 181.50 / 550 | 33.00 | 1.00 | D     | pass       | 8    |
| S07     | 90, 90, absent, 90, 90, 45     | 405 / 550    | 73.64 | 0.00 | F     | fail       | 9    |
| S08     | 60, 32.5, 60, 60, 60, 30       | 302.50 / 550 | 55.00 | 0.00 | F     | fail       | 10   |
| S10     | 50, 50, 50, 50, 50, 16.49      | 266.49 / 550 | 48.45 | 0.00 | F     | fail       | 11   |
| S11     | 85, no mark, 80, 80, 80, 40    | 365 / 450    | 81.11 | —    | —     | incomplete | —    |

Working: S02 = (4 + 4 + 5 + 5 + 5 + 5) / 6 = 4.667 → 4.67; S05 = (4 + 3.5 × 5) / 6 = 3.583 → 3.58 (79.5 is an A, not rounded to 80); S06 = (3.5 × 4 + 3) / 5 = 3.40 over the five non-exempt papers; S12 = (4 + 4 + 3.5 + 3) / 4 = 3.625 → 3.63 (half up) with two papers exempt; S11 has no mark on P2, so it is incomplete: no GPA, grade or rank, and its totals cover the five marked papers. S02, S03 and S04 share 4.67; S02's higher total puts it at 2, and S03/S04 tie on GPA, total and percentage, so both are 3 and the next rank is 5 (S12). The three failures (absent, 32.5 %, 16.49 of 50) all have GPA 0.00 and are ordered by total. Class 6 B (T1 all 60 %, T2 all 70 %) ranks on its own: T2 1 (4.00), T1 2 (3.50).

**SQL and TypeScript both produce exactly this table**: pgTAP `results_eq` on all twelve rows passes, and `results.test.ts` asserts the twelve rows against the same parity block.

## 5. Database (pgTAP)

CI `db`: see the PR's run on the review batch; `55_results.sql` has **40** assertions (37/37 on run 36199090885 before the batch).

- guards: `MARKS_NOT_LOCKED` during marks entry; `FORBIDDEN` for the class teacher, staff and another school's owner;
- a missing mark: with T2's P6 and S11's P2 unmarked, compute still runs (`{computed 14, passed 9, failed 3, incomplete 2}`); T2 is `incomplete` with no GPA or rank and T1 keeps rank 1; once T2's mark arrives the recompute gives `{14, 10, 3, 1}`;
- the golden fixture: the twelve 6 A rows match row by row; five paper lines pinned (79.5 → A, 32.5 → F, 16.50 of 50 → D passed, 16.49 → F failed, absent → 0 %/F/failed), the exempt line and the not-yet-marked line empty; 84 lines; 6 B ranked on its own; one `results.computed` event per run;
- recompute: the same summary, identical rows, 84 lines, one more event;
- RLS: the class teacher of 6 A reads 12 results and 72 lines, the class teacher of 6 B reads 2 and none of 6 A's (so cannot print a 6 A card), and nothing once 6 B is archived; a teacher of another school reads no results or lines, staff read 14, a parent and another school's owner read 0; no direct INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE even for the owner;
- read-only: `PLAN_READ_ONLY`; going back to marks entry clears results and lines and logs one `results.cleared` event with the count (14); a result cannot pair another school with school A's exam (composite FK).

`12_function_grants_invariant.sql` allow-lists `public.compute_results`.

## 8. Known issues

1. A student with a paper not yet marked is `incomplete` (no GPA, no rank); the "force-treat missing as absent" action is Part 4. Publishing still waits for complete marks (D-304).
2. §5.8's `student_code` is not a rank key (it would break every tie); §5.6's pass rule is "pass marks AND not a fail band"; both sections of the spec are rewritten to match (D-305 items 3–4).
3. No `grade_rank`, `published`, `frozen_payload` or parent read (Part 7); `gpa_without_optional` and `optional_fourth` exist but are never set (Part 6).
4. No idempotency key or rate limit on `computeResults` (recompute is idempotent by construction); no GPA distribution chart, search or "incomplete" filter on the preview.
5. The report-card seam reads real results now (#63 merged first); the fixture and the `/app/reports` sample button are gone, and a "Report card" button sits in each student's row on the preview. A student without a roll number still gets a card ("—"); with no attendance taken the percentage prints "—" and is not flagged. The card itself was not rendered live (the live database has no results until this migration ships).
6. The live journey runs in neither CI nor locally until OQ-27; the screen was verified through the preview route and unit tests.
