## 1. Scope

A paper's teacher submits their marks (warned, not blocked, about missing students); an owner or admin sees every paper's progress on the exam page and locks a submitted paper, or unlocks it with a reason — which, on a marks-locked exam, sends the exam back to marks entry and clears its results. Each paper has entry dates: outside them a teacher cannot change marks, and an owner/admin can only with a reason, recorded and stamped. The offline queue is F-ID-11 Part 3 (D-71).

## 7. Security checks

The three new functions are SECURITY DEFINER with `search_path = ''`, check the caller's role in the given workspace first (`FORBIDDEN`), and look the paper up only inside that workspace (`PAPER_NOT_FOUND`), so another school learns nothing. Unlock's reason and the late reason are length-checked (≤ 500). The server actions check `marks.write` / `marks.lock` and `requireWritable` before the RPC.
