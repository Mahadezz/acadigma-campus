## 1. Scope

**What this Part is.** A verified user who clicked "Create a school" on the chooser now reaches a real, two-step wizard instead of a "Coming soon" badge: step 1 collects the school's name, EIIN (optional, checked live against every other school on the platform), medium and education board; step 2 collects the timezone (defaulted to Asia/Dhaka, with the browser's own zone offered as a suggestion), the Sat-first working-week picker (Sat-Thu preselected) and the academic year's name and date range. Every "Continue" saves to `onboarding_progress` before advancing, so closing the tab and reopening resumes exactly where the user left off. Steps 3-5 (classes, logo, review) and the actual `app.create_school_workspace()` transaction are Part 4's — this Part stops at an honest "more on the way" screen rather than faking a step that does not exist yet.

**Acceptance criteria covered** (from spec §9 — this Part covers a slice of AC5 and AC6; the rest belong to Parts 1, 2 and 4-5):

| #   | Criterion                                                                                                           | Covered by                                                                                                                                              |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| AC5 | A user mid-wizard at step 3 who closes the browser and returns resumes with the draft intact (this Part: steps 1-2) | `apps/web/e2e/journeys/create-school-wizard.spec.ts` (live-gated); `packages/db/src/repositories/onboarding.test.ts` (`saveOnboardingDraft` round-trip) |
| AC6 | An EIIN already registered to another school shows an inline error at step 1 and does not advance                   | `supabase/tests/22_school_eiin_availability.sql`; `packages/db/src/repositories/school.test.ts`; wired in `wizard.tsx`'s `handleStep1`                  |

**Out of scope for this Part** — and where it is handled instead:

- Grade-level presets, the logo step, the review step and `app.create_school_workspace()` — Part 4.
- The join-with-code path and the first-run checklist — Part 5.
- A real Postgres enum for `board`/`medium` — both stay Zod-only (contracts) over the existing plain `text` columns; not requested by this Part's spec line.

**Risk areas** — where the testing effort went:

- The EIIN availability probe: it has to answer "does anyone already have this EIIN" for a caller who is a member of nothing, without leaking which school holds it or any other column — a `SECURITY DEFINER` boolean-only function, proven both for the "yes it's taken" and "the caller still can't read that row directly" cases in the same pgTAP file (D-66).
- Draft persistence math: `saveOnboardingDraft` merges each step's fields onto the accumulated draft and re-validates the whole thing against `createSchoolDraftSchema` — a schema mistake here would either silently drop a field on the second step's save or reject a perfectly good partial draft (§10: "a partially filled draft never fails validation").
- The `""` vs `undefined` gap on a controlled EIIN input: a plain reuse of `createSchoolStep1Schema` as the form resolver rejected a correctly-left-blank field (a real bug, caught locally before push — see Known issues).
- `noUncheckedIndexedAccess` on the day-label lookup: a `Record<number, string>` typed the values as possibly `undefined` under this repo's strict tsconfig; fixed by keying the record on the exact literal union instead (caught by `pnpm typecheck`, not a runtime bug).

### Notable cases proven

- `validateAcademicYearRange`: accepts 1 day and exactly 730 days (the boundary values), rejects `ends_on <= starts_on` and 731 days.
- `deriveFirstDayOfWeek`: returns the earliest Sat-first day regardless of the input array's own order; `null` on an empty array.
- `createSchoolDraftSchema`: a step-1-only partial draft, a partial `academic_year` (name typed, no dates yet), a malformed EIIN rejected even mid-draft, and an unknown key (Part 4's future `grade_levels`) passed through untouched.
- `checkEiinAvailability` repository: maps the RPC's boolean straight through, and an RPC error to `dependency_unavailable`.
- `saveOnboardingDraft`: now always sends `completed_at: null` in its upsert payload (D-60 follow-up fix), proven both by the exact-payload test and a dedicated regression test.
- `OnboardingShell`: `onBack` renders a `<button>` (never a `<a>`) and wins over `backHref` when both are supplied.
