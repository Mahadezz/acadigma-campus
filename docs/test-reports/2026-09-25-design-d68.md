## 1. Scope

**What this Part is.** The owner's "Blend" decision (2026-09-25): Campus keeps the acadigma.com ink/paper colours and fonts and takes the Vercel reference's details — 6px radius, hairline 1px rings instead of drop shadows on cards and popovers, lighter tightly tracked headings, a 16px body and a mono uppercase eyebrow label. The real Acadigma grid marks come into Campus: a static `GridMark` + `Logo` in `packages/ui` (geometry ported from acadigma-website, not redrawn), the Campus logo on sign-in, the onboarding chooser and the school shell's top bar, and the brand repo's Campus exports as the favicon, SVG icon, Apple touch icon, PWA icons and Open Graph image.

**Covered by:**

| What                                                  | Covered by                                                                                                             |
| ----------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Campus mark geometry equals the brand export          | `packages/ui/src/primitives/logo.test.tsx` — rect-by-rect against `campus-mark-black.svg`                              |
| Logo lockup text, muted product name, decorative mark | `logo.test.tsx`                                                                                                        |

| All five product marks render, axe clean              | `e2e/design-smoke.spec.ts` (new "Logo" section on `/design`)                                                           |
| Contrast still ≥ 4.5:1 text / 3:1 UI after the change | `node scripts/check-contrast-tokens.mjs` — 65 checks PASS (no colour token changed)                                    |
| Icons served                                          | local `next start`: `/favicon.ico` 200 `image/x-icon`; `<link rel=icon>` svg, apple-touch-icon 180, `og:image` present |

**Out of scope:** a product switcher (Campus has none yet — `GridMark`/`PRODUCT_NAMES` are ready for it); design passes on individual shipped screens (the design lane's next queue item, dashboard first); the create-school wizard's own markup (identity lane).

**Risk areas:** every card/popover/menu changes edge treatment at once; Bengali letter-spacing (headings gained tighter tracking); the school top bar at 360px now holding the switcher, a logo and the bell.
