## Session mechanics learned today

- `claude --teleport` is one-way cloud→terminal and makes a copy; Remote Control (`claude --remote-control`) is the two-way option (terminal + app on one session). Docs: code.claude.com/docs/en/claude-code-on-the-web, /remote-control.
- Never `pkill -f "<pattern>"` where the pattern appears in your own command line — it kills the shell. Kill by PID or use the `[x]` bracket trick.
- `docs-sync` CI job: any `apps/**` or `packages/**` change needs a `docs/**` change or `docs: none — <reason>` in the PR body; every new doc must be listed in `docs/README.md`.
