## 1. Purpose

Every teacher in Bangladesh has a second job: private tuition. The personal workspace is the part of Acadigma Campus that belongs to the **teacher**, not to any school — their tuition students, the attendance and fees for those students, their private file vault, their diary, their CV, the schools they belong to or have been invited to, and the document requests employers make of them. It exists for a solo tutor with no school at all, and for a school teacher who wants one place for their own material that no principal can read. "Done" from the user's chair: a teacher adds eight tuition students on her phone, marks their attendance in under thirty seconds a day, keeps her certificates in a vault she is genuinely the only reader of, and applies to a job with a CV she did not have to retype.

The Base44 prototype built most of these screens and undermined each of them. The personal shell was reachable by **any** authenticated user regardless of workspace type (W16) and had **no navigation at all on mobile** — just a 14 px header bar with two icons (§6.2, D16). Personal attendance saved by deleting every record for the day and recreating them one by one in a sequential loop with no transaction, so a mid-loop failure lost the day (row 41); the monthly summary pulled _every_ record the teacher had ever created and filtered by a month string in JavaScript (row 42). The file vault's optimistic push omitted the created `id`, so a just-uploaded file could not be deleted until reload (W9), and its promise that files are "never shared with schools or visible to anyone else" rested entirely on a `teacher_id` RLS rule with no app-level enforcement (B15). CV & Jobs toasted _"Your CV has been sent to the school"_ while sending nothing (B7) and offered "Download as PDF" that was `window.print()` of the entire page including the sidebar (B8). The invitations inbox was permanently empty because nothing ever created an invitation (D9). The seller-earnings tile was a hardcoded `৳0` (B16). Delete Account, Change Password and Connect-Google were three-step ceremonies ending in nothing (B4–B6). The rebuild keeps the product idea exactly and rebuilds every mechanism; account-level concerns move to F-ID-01 and F-ID-02, and selling moves out entirely to `/sell` (PRODUCT-DECISIONS §1.8).

## 2. Roles and permissions

A personal workspace has exactly one member, who is always `owner`. There is no sharing, no invitation, no join code (F-ID-03 §5). Permissions are therefore about **who may never read this data**, which is the more important half.

| Action                                        | The owner        | Any other user                                                                                    | A school the owner belongs to | Platform staff                                  |
| --------------------------------------------- | ---------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| Read/write tuition students, attendance, fees | ✅               | ❌                                                                                                | ❌                            | ❌ (support sees metadata counts only, F-ID-08) |
| Read/write diary entries                      | ✅               | ❌                                                                                                | ❌                            | ❌                                              |
| Upload/read/delete personal files             | ✅               | ❌                                                                                                | ❌                            | ❌                                              |
| Read/write the CV (`teacher_profiles`)        | ✅               | only if `open_to_work` and the reader is a school with the Hiring module (PRODUCT-DECISIONS §6.2) | ✅ when opted in              | ✅ for verification review                      |
| See a document request                        | ✅ (the subject) | the requesting school's hiring staff see its status only                                          | ✅ requester                  | ✅                                              |
| Approve / decline / revoke a document request | ✅ only          | ❌                                                                                                | ❌                            | ❌                                              |
| See the documents a request unlocked          | —                | the requesting school's hiring staff, while the approval is live                                  | ✅                            | ✅                                              |
| See my memberships and invitations            | ✅               | ❌                                                                                                | —                             | ✅                                              |
| Leave a school                                | ✅               | —                                                                                                 | —                             | —                                               |

Permission keys: `personal.students.write`, `personal.attendance.write`, `personal.files.write`, `personal.diary.write`, `cv.write`, `document_requests.respond`. All of them evaluate to "the caller owns this personal workspace", which `can()` expresses as a role check against the single `owner` membership — the same code path as everything else, deliberately, so there is no special case to get wrong.

## 3. Data

**Tenant key: `workspace_id`** on every table below, pointing at the personal workspace (PRODUCT-DECISIONS §1.6 — no `teacher_id` columns anywhere). Columns **proposed; DATA-MODEL.md wins**.

### `personal_students`

`id` · `workspace_id` · `full_name` · `guardian_name` · `guardian_phone` · `grade_level_label` text (free text — a tutor's "Class 9" is not a school's `grade_levels` row) · `subjects text[]` · `fee_amount bigint` (paisa) · `fee_cycle enum(monthly|per_session|package)` · `start_date` · `status enum(active|paused|ended)` · `notes` · `deleted_at` (soft delete — users expect undo here, ARCHITECTURE §4) · timestamps. Index `(workspace_id, status, full_name)`.

### `personal_attendance`

`id` · `workspace_id` · `personal_student_id` · `date date` · `status enum(present|absent|late|excused)` · `minutes int null` · `note` · `created_by` · timestamps. **Unique `(workspace_id, personal_student_id, date)`** — this single constraint is what makes the prototype's delete-all-then-recreate loop unnecessary: saving a day is one `insert … on conflict do update` statement.

### `personal_fee_payments` (proposed)

`id` · `workspace_id` · `personal_student_id` · `period_month date` (first of month) · `amount bigint` · `paid_on date` · `method enum(cash|bkash|nagad|bank|other)` · `note` · timestamps. Unique `(workspace_id, personal_student_id, period_month)` for monthly cycles. This is a ledger a tutor keeps, **not** a payment integration — no SSLCommerz, no reconciliation.

### `diary_entries`

`id` · `workspace_id` · `entry_date date` · `title` · `body text` · `mood enum(good|ok|hard)` null · `tags text[]` · `linked_student_id` null · `is_private boolean default true` · `deleted_at` · timestamps. Index `(workspace_id, entry_date desc)`, GIN on `to_tsvector(title||body)` for search. The prototype's `DiaryEntry` never wrote `workspace_id` at all (W8); here it is `not null`.

### `files` (shared table, ARCHITECTURE §4 / PRODUCT-DECISIONS §6.5)

`id` · `workspace_id` · `owner_id` · `bucket` · `path` · `name` · `mime` · `size_bytes` · `visibility enum(private|workspace|public)` · `folder text` · `checksum` · `deleted_at` · timestamps. Personal-vault files are always `visibility='private'` and live under `private/{workspace_id}/{file_id}`. Downloads go through `/api/files/[id]` and are recorded in `file_access_log`.

### `teacher_profiles` (the CV; PRODUCT-DECISIONS §6.2)

`user_id` PK (user-owned, **not** workspace-scoped — a CV follows the person) · `headline` · `summary` · `subjects text[]` · `grade_levels text[]` · `curricula text[]` · `years_experience` · `current_status enum(employed|open|freelance|student)` · `open_to_work boolean` · `expected_salary_min/max bigint` · `preferred_districts text[]` · `education jsonb[]` (institution, degree, year, result) · `experience jsonb[]` (school, role, from, to, description) · `certificate_file_ids uuid[]` (private files) · `public_link` · `verified_at`, `verified_by` (platform staff) · `profile_score smallint` · timestamps.

### `document_requests` / `document_request_items` (PRODUCT-DECISIONS §1.15)

`document_requests`: `id` · `workspace_id` (the **requesting school's** workspace — this is school data about a candidate) · `subject_user_id` (the candidate) · `application_id` null · `requested_by` · `message` · `status enum(pending|approved|declined|revoked|expired)` · `approved_at` · `expires_at` · `declined_at`, `revoked_at` · timestamps.
`document_request_items`: `id` · `request_id` · `doc_type enum(nid|passport|birth_certificate|degree|transcript|certificate|experience_letter|photo|other)` · `label` · `file_id` null (set by the candidate at approval) · `item_status enum(pending|provided|skipped)`.

### RLS, in words

- `personal_students`, `personal_attendance`, `personal_fee_payments`, `diary_entries`: the standard tenant policy against a **personal** workspace — `app.has_role(workspace_id,'{owner}')` for select, insert, update and delete. Because a personal workspace has exactly one member, this is equivalent to "only me", but it is expressed in the same helper as every other table so the pgTAP template covers it. **No platform-admin read policy** on `diary_entries` or `personal_students` — support does not read a teacher's private notes or a child's tuition record.
- `files`: select where `app.has_role(workspace_id, …)` **and** (`visibility <> 'private'` or `owner_id = app.current_user_id()`); insert/update/delete by the owner; platform staff have **no** read policy on `private` files — the only way for staff to see one is an owner-granted support access grant (F-ID-08 §4.5) and every such read is logged.
- `teacher_profiles`: select by the owner always; by any authenticated user where `open_to_work = true` **and** the reader has an active membership with `hiring.read` in a workspace whose plan includes Hiring; by platform staff for verification. Update by the owner only; `verified_at`/`verified_by` writable only by platform staff.
- `document_requests`: select by the requesting workspace's `{owner,admin}` + the requester + the **subject** (`subject_user_id = app.current_user_id()`); insert by the requesting workspace's hiring staff; update of `status` to `approved`/`declined`/`revoked` **only** by the subject; update to `revoked` also by the requester (cancelling their own ask). No delete.
- `document_request_items.file_id`: readable by the requesting workspace's hiring staff **only while** the parent request is `approved` and `expires_at > now()`. The signed-URL issuer re-checks this on every download; approval expiry is enforced at read time, not only by the sweep job.

**Private files:** the entire vault, all certificates, all identity documents, and any file attached to a document request. Every one is `private`, served only through 5-minute signed URLs from `/api/files/[id]`, and every issuance is written to `file_access_log` with the requester, the reason (`owner`, `document_request:{id}`, `support_grant:{id}`) and the correlation id. This is the app-level enforcement the prototype's B15 promise lacked.

## 4. Workflows

### 4.1 Personal dashboard

**Route:** `/personal`. **Phone:** a single column of cards in priority order — (1) _Today_ (students with a session today, with a one-tap "Mark attendance"), (2) _This month_ (attendance %, fees collected vs expected, from real queries), (3) _Invitations_ (F-ID-04 §4.7, hidden when empty), (4) _My schools_ (membership chips), (5) _Quick actions_ (Add student · New diary entry · Upload file). **Desktop:** the same cards in a two-column grid.

Every number comes from a real query (PRODUCT-DECISIONS §3.9). There is **no** seller-earnings tile here — selling lives at `/sell` and is reached from the profile menu (PRODUCT-DECISIONS §1.1); the personal dashboard shows a single quiet "You have a seller account" row only when `seller_profiles` exists, linking out.

**Empty state:** a first-run card — "Add your first tuition student" — with one primary button, replacing all five cards until a student exists.

### 4.2 Tuition students

**Route:** `/personal/students`. Phone: a `DataList` of student cards (name, class label, subjects, fee, status chip), a sticky search field, a filter sheet (status, subject), and a FAB to add. Desktop: table plus a right detail panel.

- **Add / edit:** a `FormSheet` — name (required), guardian name and phone, class label, subjects (chips), fee amount + cycle, start date, notes. Saves with `upsertPersonalStudent`.
- **Detail:** attendance history (last 30 days as a compact strip), fee ledger, notes, and Pause / End / Delete.
- **Delete:** soft delete with a 10-second undo snackbar; a deleted student's attendance rows are retained and hidden.

**Audit:** `personal_student.created/updated/deleted`. **Notifications:** none — nobody else is involved.
**Failure cases:** duplicate name is allowed (two students can share a name) but the sheet warns; deleting a student with unpaid fees warns first.

### 4.3 Personal attendance

**Route:** `/personal/attendance`. This screen is the one a teacher uses daily on a phone, so it is specified tightly.

**Phone:** a date strip at the top (seven days, today centred, swipeable), then one row per active student: name on the left and a four-segment status control (P / A / L / E) on the right, each segment ≥ 44 px. Tapping a segment marks immediately and optimistically; there is **no Save button** — the screen autosaves with a debounce of 800 ms and shows a single "Saved" pill. A "Mark all present" button sits at the bottom in thumb reach and is the fastest path (one tap plus exceptions).

**Save mechanics:** one `insert … on conflict (workspace_id, personal_student_id, date) do update` per batch, sent as a single statement — not the prototype's delete-all-then-loop. Offline, the mutation queues in IndexedDB with an idempotency key and replays on reconnect (ARCHITECTURE §6).

**Monthly view:** a month grid (students × days) rendered from a **server-side aggregate** (`personal_attendance_monthly(workspace_id, month)`), never by pulling every row and filtering in JavaScript. Phone shows one student per row with a horizontally scrollable day strip and a % column pinned right.

**Rules:** `late` and `excused` count as present for the percentage by default, matching the school-side policy (PRODUCT-DECISIONS §2.2) with a personal toggle in settings.
**Audit:** `personal_attendance.saved` with the date and the count of rows changed (not per-student rows — that would flood the trail).
**Failure cases:** a student added mid-day appears immediately without desyncing the loaded records (the prototype's W10 bug is avoided by keying the query on the date alone and rendering the roster from a separate live query); an offline save that conflicts on replay resolves last-write-wins per (student, date) and tells the user which days were merged.

### 4.4 Fees ledger

Inside a student's detail and as a `/personal/students?tab=fees` summary. One row per month per student: expected, paid, date, method, a Mark-paid action. The monthly card on the dashboard is the sum. No payment processing, no reminders by SMS in v1 (no provider — F-ID-01 OQ-1); a "Copy reminder message" button produces text the teacher can paste into WhatsApp, and logs a `contact_log`-style row so the teacher remembers they asked.

### 4.5 File vault

**Route:** `/personal/files`. Folders are **user-created**, not the prototype's five fixed ones; a new vault seeds with four suggested folders (Certificates, Lesson material, Student work, Personal) that can be renamed or deleted.

- **Upload:** phone picker (camera, gallery, files) → client-side type and size check → `createFileUploadUrl` → direct-to-storage upload → `confirmFileUpload` returns the **canonical row including the `id`** and the list is invalidated (the prototype's W9 bug — an optimistic push without an id — cannot happen because the optimistic entry carries a temporary id that is replaced by the server row).
- **Preview:** images and PDFs render inline through a 5-minute signed URL; everything else downloads.
- **Delete:** soft delete with undo, then the storage object is removed by the nightly sweep.
- **Quota:** the storage meter is real — `sum(files.size_bytes)` for the workspace against `plans.storage_gb` (PRODUCT-DECISIONS §3.11). At quota, upload is blocked with an upgrade prompt, not a silent failure.
- **The privacy promise:** the screen says "Only you can open these files." The screen also links to a one-line explanation of _why that is true_ (private bucket, signed URLs, no school policy, every access logged) — because the prototype made the same claim with nothing behind it.

**Audit:** `file.uploaded`, `file.deleted`, and `file_access_log` rows on every signed-URL issuance.
**Failure cases:** upload interrupted → the storage object is orphaned and swept after 24 h with no `files` row; wrong MIME by magic bytes → rejected before the row is written; over quota → blocked with the exact numbers shown.

### 4.6 Diary

**Route:** `/personal/diary`. A reverse-chronological list of entries with a date, title and snippet; full-text search; a compose `FormSheet` with date, title, body, optional mood and tags, and an optional link to a tuition student. Entries are private by definition — there is no sharing control to get wrong. Editing keeps `updated_at`; deleting is a soft delete with undo.

**Phone:** compose opens as a full-height sheet with the body field focused and the keyboard-safe Save pinned. A "Today" quick-entry chip on the dashboard writes straight into a new entry.
**Audit:** `diary_entry.created/updated/deleted` — **field names only, never content.**

### 4.7 CV (teacher profile)

**Route:** `/personal/cv`. Sections: headline and summary, subjects and grade levels, curricula, experience (repeatable), education (repeatable), certificates (private files picked from the vault), expected salary and preferred districts, and an **Open to work** switch with a plain-language explanation of exactly who can see the profile when it is on.

- **Completeness score** (`profile_score`) is shown as a ring with the next missing item named: "Add one experience entry — +15 %". The formula is in §5, not invented per render (the prototype computed a completeness % ad hoc).
- **Download as PDF** renders server-side through `/api/pdf/cv` with the Bengali font embedded (ARCHITECTURE §5) — a real, one-page, print-clean document, not `window.print()` of the page (fixing B8).
- **Applying to a job** is the Hiring area's flow (`/jobs/:slug/apply`); this screen only guarantees the CV is complete and offers "Find jobs". The prototype's false "Your CV has been sent" toast (B7) has no equivalent here — the hiring area owns the confirmation and it is backed by a real application row plus a notification.
- **Verification:** requesting verification of degrees/identity submits to the platform KYC-style queue (F-ID-08); the badge appears only when `verified_at` is set.

**Audit:** `teacher_profile.updated`, `teacher_profile.open_to_work_changed` (this one matters — it changes who can read the row).

### 4.8 Document requests — consent

**Route:** `/personal/requests` (PRODUCT-DECISIONS §1.15).

1. A school's hiring staff creates a request naming the documents they want (NID, degree certificate, experience letter…) and an optional message. The candidate receives `document_request.received` in-app and by email.
2. The candidate's screen shows **who is asking, which school, which application, exactly which documents, and for how long**. Per item they can attach a file from the vault, upload a new one, or mark it skipped.
3. **Approve** sets `status='approved'`, `approved_at`, `expires_at = approved_at + 30 days`. Only then can the requester obtain signed URLs, and only for the items with a `file_id`.
4. **Decline** ends it with an optional reason. **Revoke** is available at any time while approved and takes effect immediately — the next signed-URL request fails.
5. A banner on the request lists every time the school downloaded each document, read from `file_access_log`. Consent you cannot audit is not consent.

**Notifications:** `document_request.received` (candidate), `document_request.approved` / `document_request.declined` / `document_request.revoked` (requester), `document_request.expiring` (candidate and requester, 3 days before expiry).
**Audit:** `document_request.created/approved/declined/revoked/expired` plus a `file_access_log` row per download.
**Failure cases:** expiry is enforced at signed-URL issuance, so a stale approval cannot leak; revoking while a download is in flight cannot be prevented, and the UI says the 5-minute URL window honestly.

### 4.9 My schools and invitations

**Route:** `/personal/workspaces`. Three sections: **Invitations** (F-ID-04 §4.7 — the inbox the prototype built and never fed), **My schools** (one card per membership with the school name, role, status and a Switch button; pending memberships show "Waiting for approval" with Cancel), and **Join a school with a code**.

**Leave a school:** a destructive confirm sheet naming the consequences (access ends now; your school-library resources will be handed to an admin to reassign — PRODUCT-DECISIONS §3.7; your personal workspace is untouched) → `leaveWorkspace` (F-ID-03 §4.6). The prototype deleted the member row and reloaded the page, and only ever found the _first_ school; here every membership is listed and removal is a status change.

### 4.10 Personal settings

**Route:** `/personal/settings`. Tabs: **Profile** and **Appearance** and **Notifications** (all F-ID-02), **Security** (F-ID-01: password, phone, devices, delete account), **Workspace** (rename the personal workspace, attendance-percentage policy toggle, storage meter). Every one of the prototype's dead controls on this screen is either implemented by the feature that owns it or removed — nothing renders that does not work.

## 9. Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** any second user or any school admin, **when** they query `personal_students`, `personal_attendance`, `diary_entries` or a private `files` row belonging to another person's personal workspace, **then** zero rows are returned — including for a platform admin.
2. **Given** a personal-only account on a 360×800 phone, **when** they open the app, **then** a five-item bottom nav is present and every destination is reachable with one thumb.
3. **Given** a teacher marking attendance for 12 students, **when** they tap "Mark all present" and change two to absent, **then** exactly one upsert statement is sent, 12 rows exist for that date, and no row was ever deleted.
4. **Given** two devices saving the same date within a second, **when** both writes land, **then** each `(student, date)` has exactly one row and no day is lost.
5. **Given** attendance marked while offline, **when** the device reconnects, **then** the queued mutation replays with its idempotency key, the user sees a sync confirmation naming the days, and no duplicate rows are created.
6. **Given** a month with 30 students and 26 marked days, **when** the month view loads, **then** the data comes from the server-side aggregate in one request and no client-side filtering of a full table occurs.
7. **Given** a file just uploaded, **when** the user taps delete **before any reload**, **then** the delete succeeds because the list row carries the server-issued `id` (the prototype's W9 bug).
8. **Given** a private vault file, **when** its signed URL is requested, **then** the URL expires in 5 minutes, a `file_access_log` row records the reason `owner`, and no unsigned path to the object exists.
9. **Given** a workspace at 100 % of its storage quota, **when** an upload is attempted, **then** it is blocked with the exact used/total figures and an upgrade link.
10. **Given** a CV with a headline, summary, subjects, one experience and one education entry, **when** the score is computed, **then** it equals the sum defined in §5 and the ring names the highest-value missing item.
11. **Given** `open_to_work = false`, **when** a hiring-staff user of any school queries `teacher_profiles`, **then** zero rows are returned; **when** it is switched on, **then** the row becomes readable to hiring staff whose plan includes Hiring, and to nobody else.
12. **Given** a CV download, **when** the PDF is produced, **then** it is server-rendered, one page, contains Bengali text correctly, and includes no app chrome.
13. **Given** a document request, **when** the candidate opens it, **then** the screen names the requesting school, the requester, every requested document and the 30-day window before any Approve button is reachable.
14. **Given** an approved document request, **when** the school downloads a document, **then** the download appears in the candidate's history within one refetch, attributed to the named staff member.
15. **Given** an approved document request that the candidate revokes, **when** the school requests a new signed URL, **then** it is refused with `CONSENT_EXPIRED` and the refusal is audited.
16. **Given** an approval older than 30 days, **when** a download is attempted **before** the nightly sweep has run, **then** it is still refused — expiry is enforced at issuance.
17. **Given** a diary entry, **when** `audit_events` is read, **then** the row names the changed fields and contains none of the entry's text.
18. **Given** a teacher who leaves a school from `/personal/workspaces`, **when** the action completes, **then** the membership is `removed` (not deleted), their personal workspace is untouched, and the school's owners are notified.

## 10. Tests

- **Unit:** attendance percentage with each policy toggle; fee expectation per cycle; `profileScore` matrix including the "next best item" selection; quota arithmetic and the 90 % warning threshold; file type/magic-byte validation; consent expiry evaluation; audit redaction (diary/notes never serialised).
- **DB (pgTAP):** isolation and escalation for `personal_students`, `personal_attendance`, `personal_fee_payments`, `diary_entries`, `files`, `teacher_profiles`, `document_requests`, `document_request_items` — for each: another personal-workspace owner, a school admin who shares no workspace, a school admin the user _is_ a member of, an anonymous role, and a platform admin (the last must read **nothing** on the private tables). Consent-specific: the requester reads `document_request_items.file_id` only while `approved` and unexpired; the subject can always read; `status` can be set to `approved` only by the subject.
- **Integration:** every action in §7 for happy path and every named error; `savePersonalAttendance` idempotency replay; `confirmFileUpload` checksum mismatch; `/api/files/[id]` for all three reasons and their denials.
- **E2E (360×800 and 1280×800):** `add-students-and-mark-attendance`, `offline-attendance-replay`, `upload-preview-delete-undo`, `cv-fill-and-download-pdf`, `document-request-approve-download-revoke` (two accounts), `leave-school-from-personal`.
- **A11y:** axe on every personal route; the attendance segment control is a labelled radio group per student with keyboard support; undo snackbars are `aria-live` and are not the only route to recovery (a Trash view exists for 30 days).
- **Performance budgets:** the attendance screen for 40 students renders in ≤ 1.2 s on simulated 3G and each tap responds in < 100 ms optimistically; `getPersonalAttendanceMonth` p95 ≤ 200 ms at 40 students × 31 days; the file grid virtualises above 60 items; the dashboard issues ≤ 4 queries.
- **Security:** a test that no API returns a storage path or an unsigned URL; a Semgrep rule forbidding `visibility: 'public'` anywhere under `(personal)`; the "only you can open these files" claim is asserted by an explicit cross-account e2e that fails the build if it ever becomes false.

## 11. Open questions

- **OQ-1: fee reminders.** No SMS provider in v1 (F-ID-01 OQ-1). **Default assumed:** a "Copy reminder message" button plus a logged note; no automated reminders.
- **OQ-2: does a personal workspace have a plan?** PRODUCT-DECISIONS §5.5 says personal workspaces can buy marketplace items and credit packs but only school workspaces have subscriptions — yet the storage quota needs a `plans` row. **Default assumed:** personal workspaces are permanently on the **Free** plan for limits (1 GB, 20 AI credits/day) with no subscription row. Billing area to confirm.
- **OQ-3: tuition students vs school students.** They are deliberately separate tables with no linking. **Default assumed:** no link, ever — linking would let a school infer a teacher's private tuition roster. Recorded so it is not "helpfully" added later.
- **OQ-4: CV public link.** `teacher_profiles.public_link` is proposed but PRODUCT-DECISIONS §6.2 says schools browse only opted-in profiles and says nothing about a public URL. **Default assumed:** no public URL in v1; the column is not populated and the field is hidden.
- **OQ-5: who creates document requests.** PRODUCT-DECISIONS §1.15 places the response in the personal area but does not name the requester side. **Default assumed:** the Hiring module's staff (owner/admin, or the hiring manager on an application), specified by the ops area; this feature owns only the subject's consent surface.
- **OQ-6: platform-staff access to private personal data.** **Default assumed: none by policy**, with the owner-granted, time-boxed, fully-logged support grant in F-ID-08 §4.5 as the single exception. This is stricter than "platform admin has a bypass policy on the tables the console needs" in ARCHITECTURE §3, and deliberately so — flagged as a conflict in the area README.
