## 1. Purpose

The parent portal is the product's public face: for most families it is the only part of Acadigma Campus they will ever open, usually on a cheap Android phone, often on a slow connection, sometimes in Bengali. `/family` gives a guardian a child switcher and seven tabs — **Today · Attendance · Results · Timetable · Homework · Behaviour · Notices** — each showing exactly what PRODUCT-DECISIONS 1.13 lists for their own children and nothing else. "Done": a father opens the app, sees "Rahim was present today · Maths homework due tomorrow · Half-Yearly result published", taps once for the mark sheet, and at no point can any request, by any route, return a fact about another family's child.

**This spec owns the `/family` shell, the child switcher and the guardian-scoped read views.** Every other academics feature exposes its data to parents _through the views defined here_; none of them writes its own parent query.

**Base44 intent vs reality.** The prototype's `/parent-portal` called `Student.list()` — **downloading every student in the workspace into the parent's browser** — and then filtered by a **case-sensitive** email string match in React. The network response therefore contained every other child's health data, guardian NIDs and addresses, and a parent whose account email differed in case saw "No children linked". There was no guardian entity and no invitation: `parent` was simply a role an admin could pick from a dropdown. The Attendance tab read a table that nothing in the application ever wrote, so it was permanently empty. The Marks tab used a **different grade scale** from every other screen — the same 72 % showed as "C" to the parent and "B+" to the teacher. Report cards, behaviour, timetable and messaging were not exposed to parents at all. Extensive parent-mode branches existed on `/students` and `/students/:id`, routes parents could never reach.

## 3. Data

> Proposed; **`docs/architecture/DATA-MODEL.md` wins.** This feature introduces almost no tables of its own — its substance is the **view layer** and the **policies**.

**Owned tables**

**`parent_profile_change_requests`** — `guardian_id`, `user_id`, `student_id`, `field text` (`phone|alt_phone|email|address|occupation`), `current_value text`, `requested_value text`, `status change_request_status` (`pending|approved|rejected`), `decided_by`, `decided_at`, `decision_note`.
Index: `index (workspace_id, status)`, `index (workspace_id, guardian_id)`.

**`parent_reads`** — a lightweight read-receipt ledger so the school can see whether a result or a notice was actually opened: `user_id`, `student_id`, `entity_type text` (`result|behaviour_log|announcement|assignment`), `entity_id uuid`, `read_at timestamptz`.
Index: `unique (user_id, entity_type, entity_id)`, `index (workspace_id, entity_type, entity_id)`.

**Consumed (owned elsewhere)**: `guardian_users`, `guardians`, `students`, `enrollments` (F-AC-02) · `attendance_records`, `attendance_sessions` (F-AC-03) · `timetable_slots`, `bell_periods`, `timetable_exceptions` (F-AC-05) · `exams`, `results`, `result_subject_lines`, `report_comments` (F-AC-06) · `assignments`, `assignment_submissions`, `assignment_attachments` (F-AC-07) · `behaviour_logs` (F-AC-08) · `announcements` (F-OP-08) · `calendar_events`, `holidays` (F-AC-11) · `files`.

**The view contract (WF-02 stage D).** A parent has **no direct policy** on `sections`, `subjects`, `marks`, `attendance_records` or `timetable_slots`. Every read goes through a `security definer` view scoped by `app.is_guardian_of(student_id)`, projecting exactly the columns PRODUCT-DECISIONS 1.13 lists and nothing else:

| `parent_attendance_v`         | date, status, note, session period                                                                                     | own child; the school's own `%` rule applied by `app.attendance_pct`                          |

| `parent_result_lines_v`       | subject name, full marks, obtained, percentage, letter, grade point, `subject_kind`, status                            | same published-only filter                                                                    |
| `parent_report_comments_v`    | comment body, author display name                                                                                      | `approved = true` **and** the parent's own child **and** a published result                   |
| `parent_timetable_v`          | day, period label, start/end time, subject name, teacher display name, room name                                       | the child's section, the **published** timetable version effective today, exceptions overlaid |

| `parent_submissions_v`        | status, score, feedback, submitted date                                                                                | **own child's row only** — never a classmate's                                                |

| `parent_notices_v`            | title, body, published date, attachments                                                                               | announcements addressed to the parents of the child's section                                 |
| `parent_calendar_v`           | date, title, kind (holiday / exam / event)                                                                             | school-wide plus the child's section                                                          |

Each view is `security definer`, `search_path` pinned, owned by a role with no write grants, and **every one begins with the same `app.is_guardian_of(student_id)` predicate** — so there is one place to get the scoping right and one place to test it.

**Enums**: `change_request_status`.

**RLS in words.** `parent_profile_change_requests`: a parent may insert and select **their own** rows; owner/admin may select and decide all. `parent_reads`: a parent may insert and select only rows with their own `user_id`; owner/admin may select all (aggregated as "opened by 28 of 38 families"). No parent-role policy exists anywhere on `student_risk_scores`, `student_flags`, `risk_interventions`, `staff_attendance`, `leave_requests`, `marks` (raw), `guardians` of other students, or any private file that is not their child's — asserted against `pg_policies` by a pgTAP test, not merely by behaviour. A parent membership with `status <> 'active'` loses everything instantly because every helper checks it (PRODUCT-DECISIONS 1.14).

**Private files.** A parent may fetch, through `GET /api/files/[id]`: their child's photo, their child's mark-sheet and ID-card PDFs, student-facing assignment attachments, announcement attachments, and any file the school explicitly shared with them. Everything else 403s with an access-log entry. Health records, guardian NIDs and question papers are never reachable.

## 4. Workflows

**4.1 First sign-in and child linking.** Covered end-to-end by F-AC-02 §4.3 and WF-02: the school invites a guardian, they accept at `/invite/[token]`, a `parent` membership and `guardian_users` rows are created. This feature's part is the landing: `resolveLandingRoute` sends a user whose active workspace membership is `parent` to `/family`, and middleware plus RLS make `/app/*` unreachable (a hand-crafted server action returns `FORBIDDEN`; RLS returns zero rows).

**4.2 The shell and child switcher.**
_Trigger:_ the parent opens the app.
_Steps:_ `/family` resolves `parent_children_v`. **One child → no switcher**, the shell goes straight to that child's Today tab. **Several children → a horizontal row of 64 px cards** (photo, first name, class) pinned under the top bar; the selection is stored in a cookie so the app reopens where they left off. Tabs sit in a scrollable strip on phone and a left rail at ≥1024.
_Outcome:_ `/family/[childId]?tab=today`.
_Failures:_ zero linked children (the invitation was revoked, or the student withdrew) → a plain "No children are linked to this account. Contact the school." page with the school's phone number from `school_profiles` — never an empty dashboard and never a permission-shaped error.

**4.3 Today.** The tab that earns the daily open: today's attendance status, the next two periods with room and teacher, homework due today and tomorrow, any result published in the last 7 days, unread notices, and the next calendar event. Each card links into its tab. Nothing on it is computed in the browser.

**4.4 Attendance.** A month grid (P/A/L/E/H, non-school days greyed from F-AC-11), the percentage ring computed by the **same** `app.attendance_pct` the teacher's screen uses, a list of non-present days with notes, and a link to "how attendance is counted at this school" showing the school's own policy in plain language (late counts as present, etc.). _The prototype computed this tab with a different formula from the teacher's screen and, in any case, read an empty table._

**4.5 Results.** A list of **published** exams only, newest first. Tapping one shows the mark sheet: subject lines with letter and grade point, GPA (and `gpa_without_optional` when the 4th-subject rule applies, labelled so the bonus is visible), pass/fail, rank if the school shows it, the approved teacher comment, and a **Download PDF** button that serves the same `frozen_payload` the school printed. An unpublished exam is **invisible** — never shown as zero or "pending marks" (WF-02 stage D). Opening a result writes a `parent_reads` row.

**4.6 Timetable.** The child's weekly routine from the published version, in the school's own working-day order (Sat–Thu by default), with today highlighted and exceptions overlaid ("Period 3 today: substituted"). An **Add to calendar** button produces the child's ICS from F-AC-05 via a signed, revocable subscription URL.

**4.7 Homework.** Three groups, **Missing first** because that is what a parent needs: Missing · Due this week · Past. Each row shows subject, title, due date, the child's status, score and teacher feedback, plus any student-facing attachment. When `submission_mode = 'file_upload'`, an **Upload** button lets the guardian submit on the child's behalf (students have no accounts in v1 — PRODUCT-DECISIONS 1.22); the file is private, the teacher is notified.

**4.8 Behaviour.** Only logs with `parent_visible = true` and not retracted, newest first, as cards: date, category, positive/negative, points, description, teacher. Opening the tab stamps `parent_seen_at` on the visible logs, which is what locks those logs against silent editing (F-AC-08 §5.8). No leaderboard, no rank, no other child.

**4.9 Notices.** Announcements addressed to the parents of that child's section (F-OP-08), with attachments, read state and the school calendar's upcoming events.

**4.10 Contact-detail change request.** A parent taps "My details" → edits a phone or address → **Request update**. An admin sees it in a queue and applies or rejects it with a note. Audit `guardian.change_requested` / `.approved` / `.rejected`. _A parent cannot directly rewrite the number the school dials in an emergency._

**4.11 Revoke own access.** "Remove my access to this child" sets `guardian_users.status = 'revoked'`; access dies on the next request because every view checks `status = 'active'`. Admins are notified. Re-linking requires a new invitation.

**4.12 Notifications a parent receives.** `attendance.absent`, `result.published`, `assignment.published`, `assignment.missing` (at close), `behaviour.shared`, `announcement.published`, `exam.upcoming`, plus digests when more than three land in a day. Every one carries an `action_url` into the right tab (PRODUCT-DECISIONS 1.11). A parent never receives a risk notification.

**4.13 Phone and locale specifics.** 360×800 is the design target and the realistic device. The shell is bottom-tab-free (the tab strip is under the child switcher) so the thumb zone is free for content; the switcher and tabs are horizontally scrollable; every list is cursor-paginated server-side. Language follows the parent's own `user_preferences` — the school may seed a default but never overrides it (WF-02 §11); Bengali is a first-class locale with the same font stack the PDFs embed. The portal is fully usable offline-first for **reads** (the last-fetched tab is cached by the service worker with a "showing data from 09:12" stamp), because a parent on a Dhaka bus is the normal case.

## 5. Business rules and calculations

1. **One scoping predicate.** Every parent-facing read resolves through `app.is_guardian_of(student_id)`, which is true only when a `guardian_users` row exists for `(auth.uid(), student_id)` with `status = 'active'` **and** the corresponding `workspace_members` row is `status = 'active'`. There is no email matching anywhere in the product; the prototype's case-sensitivity bug is structurally impossible.
2. **No client-side filtering, ever.** Every list is server-filtered and cursor-paginated. A test asserts that no `/family` network response contains a `student_id` the signed-in user is not linked to — the single most important regression test in this feature, because the prototype shipped the opposite.
3. **Published-only.** Results, exams and announcements are visible only in their published state; assignments additionally require `parent_visible`; behaviour logs additionally require `parent_visible` and non-retraction. "Not yet published" renders as absence, never as a zero.
4. **One grade scale, one percentage.** The portal calls `app.band_for` and `app.attendance_pct` — the same functions the teacher's screens use — and never re-implements a band table or an average. A parity test compares one fixture student's portal payload against the teacher-side payload field by field.
5. **Frozen results.** A published result renders from `frozen_payload` when present (F-AC-06 §5.14), so a later subject rename or grade-scale edit cannot change what a family was shown. If a result is republished, the parent is notified that it changed.
6. **Rank visibility** is governed by `grading.show_rank_to_parents` (default on). When off, the rank field is omitted from `parent_results_v` itself, not hidden in the UI.
7. **Read receipts.** Opening a result, notice or shared behaviour log writes a `parent_reads` row (idempotent on the unique index). The school sees aggregates ("28 of 38 families opened the result"), never a "last seen" timestamp presented as surveillance of a family.
8. **Contact changes are requests.** `parent_profile_change_requests` never writes to `guardians` directly; an admin's approval applies the value in one audited action.
9. **Access dies with the link.** Setting `guardian_users.status` to `revoked`, or the `parent` membership to `removed`, cuts every view on the next request. No cache or token extends it beyond the current request.
10. **Multiple guardians, one child** each see the same data; **one guardian, several children** switches between them. A guardian linked to children in two different schools has two `parent` memberships and switches workspace first — the child switcher never crosses a workspace boundary.
11. **Sibling privacy.** A parent linked to child A and not child B sees nothing of B, even when B is in the same section and appears in the same class announcement — announcements are projected without recipient lists.
12. **Timezone and locale.** All dates render in `school_profiles.timezone`; numerals and dates follow the parent's locale, with Bengali digits when the locale is `bn` and the school has not opted out.
13. **What a parent can never reach**, restated because it is a security boundary rather than a UI choice: risk scores, flags, interventions, AI suggestions, staff attendance and leave, raw `marks` rows, unapproved comments, unpublished results, non-student-facing attachments, question papers, another child's anything.

## 8. Parts (build chunks)

**Part 1 — Shell, landing and child switcher** · `/family` route group, `resolveLandingRoute` sending `parent` memberships here, middleware blocking `/app/*`, `parent_children_v`, the switcher with cookie-persisted selection, the tab strip, the zero-children state with the school's phone number · tests: pgTAP on the view (a parent sees exactly their linked, active children); e2e proving `/app/students` 303s to `/family` and a hand-crafted server action returns `FORBIDDEN` · **Demo:** a father with two children signs in, switches between them, and cannot reach any `/app` route by URL.

**Part 2 — The view layer and the isolation test suite** · all thirteen `security definer` views from §3 with the single shared `app.is_guardian_of` predicate, `search_path` pinned, no write grants; the pgTAP suite that asserts, view by view, that a parent of child A gets zero rows for child B (including a classmate in the same section and a sibling of another family) · tests: the `pg_policies` assertion that **no parent policy exists** on risk, flags, interventions, staff attendance or raw `marks`; a network-payload test asserting no `/family` response contains an unlinked `student_id` · **Demo:** the isolation suite runs green in CI and fails loudly when a view is written without the predicate.

**Part 3 — Attendance, Timetable and Today** · `parent_attendance_v` + summary using the shared `app.attendance_pct`, month grid, the "how attendance is counted here" explainer, `parent_timetable_v` with exceptions and ICS, the Today tab assembling the six cards, service-worker read caching with the stale-data badge · tests: parity test comparing the parent's percentage with the teacher's for the same child and policy; holiday greyed rather than 0 % · **Demo:** a parent sees 90.91 % and the class teacher sees 90.91 % for the same child, from one function.

**Part 4 — Results and mark sheet** · `parent_results_v` / `parent_result_lines_v` / `parent_report_comments_v` with the published-only and approved-only filters, the mark-sheet view with GPA, 4th-subject labelling and rank behind its flag, PDF download from `frozen_payload`, `result.published` notification with an `action_url` · tests: an unpublished result invisible; an unapproved comment invisible; a renamed subject not changing an issued sheet; band parity with the teacher's screen · **Demo:** the school publishes the Half-Yearly result and a parent opens the identical mark sheet the office printed.

## 9. Acceptance criteria

1. **Given** a guardian linked to two children, **when** they open `/family`, **then** a switcher shows exactly those two children and no other student appears in any network response — asserted by inspecting the payload, not the DOM.
2. **Given** a guardian linked to one child, **when** they open `/family`, **then** no switcher is rendered and they land directly on that child's Today tab.
3. **Given** a parent of child A, **when** they request child B's attendance, results, timetable, assignments, submissions, behaviour or notices by id through any endpoint, **then** the response is a **not-found**, identical in shape to a request for a non-existent id, and the underlying views return zero rows (pgTAP, per view).
4. **Given** a parent, **when** they navigate to `/app/students` or call any `/app` server action directly, **then** they are redirected to `/family`, the action returns `FORBIDDEN`, and RLS independently returns zero rows.
5. **Given** an exam whose results are not published, **when** the parent opens the Results tab, **then** the exam is **absent** — not listed as pending and not shown as zero.
6. **Given** a published result, **when** the parent opens it, **then** the GPA, letter and every subject band match the teacher's screen exactly, because both read `app.band_for` and the stored `frozen_payload`.
7. **Given** a child with 18 present, 1 late, 1 half-day and 2 absent in a month under the default policy, **when** the parent opens Attendance, **then** the percentage is `90.91` — identical to the teacher's screen and the monthly register.
8. **Given** the school changes `weights.half_day` to 0.5, **when** the parent reloads, **then** their percentage changes in step with the teacher's — one function, one answer.
9. **Given** a behaviour log with `parent_visible = false`, **when** the parent opens the Behaviour tab, **then** it is absent; **given** it is shared, **then** it appears and `parent_seen_at` is stamped, after which the teacher can no longer silently edit it (F-AC-08 §5.8).
10. **Given** an assignment attachment marked `is_student_facing = false`, **when** the parent opens the assignment, **then** the attachment is absent and a direct file request returns 403 with a `file_access_log` entry.
11. **Given** a parent, **when** they query `student_risk_scores`, `student_flags`, `risk_interventions`, `staff_attendance` or raw `marks`, **then** nothing is returned — and a pgTAP test asserts that **no parent policy exists** on those tables at all.
12. **Given** a guardian link set to `revoked`, **when** the parent makes their next request, **then** every view returns zero rows and the shell shows the "no children linked" state — no cached session extends access.
13. **Given** a parent, **when** they edit their phone number, **then** a `parent_profile_change_requests` row is created and `guardians` is **unchanged** until an admin approves it.
14. **Given** a parent whose locale is `bn`, **when** any tab renders, **then** it renders in Bengali with the shared font stack, regardless of the school's default language.
15. **Given** an assignment with `submission_mode = 'file_upload'`, **when** the parent uploads a file, **then** the submission status becomes `submitted` (or `late`), the file is private, the teacher is notified, and another family's parent requesting that file id gets 403.
16. **Given** a slow connection, **when** the parent reopens a previously loaded tab offline, **then** the cached content renders with a "showing data from 09:12" badge rather than an error or a blank screen.
17. **Given** a section announcement, **when** a parent reads it, **then** no recipient list is present in the payload — a parent cannot learn which other families are in the section.
18. **Given** a parent at 360×800, **when** they use the switcher and tab strip, **then** both scroll horizontally, all targets are ≥ 44 px, and no page scrolls horizontally.

## 10. Tests

- **Unit (`packages/domain`)**: `resolveLandingRoute` for every membership combination; `groupAssignmentsForParent` (missing/due/past boundaries in the school timezone); `todayCardAssembly`; `localeNumerals` for Bengali digits; `staleDataLabel`.
- **DB (pgTAP) — the centrepiece**: for **each of the thirteen views**, a positive test (a parent of A sees A's rows) and three negative tests (a classmate in the same section, a sibling of another family, a child in another workspace) — 52 assertions that encode the exact hole the prototype shipped. Plus: `pg_policies` assertions that no parent-role policy exists on `student_risk_scores`, `student_flags`, `risk_interventions`, `staff_attendance`, `leave_requests`, `marks`, `guardians` (of unlinked students) or `student_documents` (non-own); every view is `security definer` with a pinned `search_path` and no write grant; a revoked link returns zero rows immediately.
- **Payload test (e2e)**: for every `/family` route, intercept the network responses and assert that the set of `student_id` values appearing anywhere in them is a subset of the signed-in user's linked children — the single regression test that would have caught `ParentPortal.jsx:42-47`.
- **Parity tests**: the parent's attendance percentage vs the teacher's for the same child and policy; the parent's letter/GPA vs the teacher's for the same result; both must be field-identical, because the prototype's parent portal used a different grade scale.
- **Integration**: published/unpublished result visibility; approved/unapproved comment visibility; parent file upload and cross-family 403; change-request approval applying the value in one audited action; self-revocation.
- **E2E (360×800 and 1280×800, axe)**: `parent-first-signin-and-switch`, `parent-sees-published-result-only`, `parent-attendance-matches-teacher`, `parent-uploads-homework`, `parent-cannot-reach-app-routes`, `parent-offline-cached-tab`. Axe clean on all eight parent screens; contrast and 44 px targets verified at 360 px.
- **Performance budgets** (this is a low-end-device audience): Today tab first contentful paint < 1.8 s on a mid-range Android over 3G; every tab's payload ≤ 60 KB gzipped; each view query p95 < 200 ms; Lighthouse PWA ≥ 90 on the `/family` shell; zero client-side table downloads (asserted by a payload-size budget test).

## 11. Open questions

**Results tab status (demo cut, D-306, with F-AC-06 Part 7):** `/family` lists the signed-in parent's children's published results, newest first — child, exam, class and section, pass/fail, GPA, grade, "rank of n" and "tied", marks by subject in a native disclosure — and "Download report card" (`GET /api/family/report-card?examId&studentId`), which renders the card through the parent's own RLS client, so a parent downloads only their own child's published card and anything else is `not_found`; nothing is written, so it works in a read-only school. Everything is read from `results.frozen_payload` (§5 rule 5): the parent reads no `students`, `exams`, `marks` or `result_subject_lines` data beyond their child's published result. RLS on `results` (not the §3 views yet): published, not withheld, an active `parent` member, `app.is_guardian_of(student_id)` over the new `guardian_users` (active links only). Western digits on screen in both languages; the PDF follows the card's Bangla numerals. Permission `family.results.read` (parent). **No parent is linked to a child in the product until F-AC-02 Part 4's invite flow ships**; until then the page shows the shell's empty state. Deviations: no child switcher or tab strip (Part 1), no security-definer `parent_results_v`/`parent_result_lines_v` (Part 2 — the RLS policy is the boundary for now), no `show_rank_to_parents` flag (rank is shown), no `result.published` notification, no service-worker cache. See D-306.

1. **Should parents see the class rank?** Default on (`grading.show_rank_to_parents`), matching BD norms. Flagged for the owner — some schools consider publishing rank to families harmful, and the flag removes the column from the view itself.
2. **Two-way messaging.** v1 is read-only plus the contact log; a parent cannot message a teacher. Assumed correct for launch (PRODUCT-DECISIONS 6.7 defers SMS/WhatsApp), but it is the most likely first request. It would be an F-OP module change, with this shell adding a tab.
3. **Fees and payments in the portal.** Out of scope for v1 (the billing module is school-facing). Flagged: schools will ask, and it is the second-most-likely request.
4. **Read receipts.** Aggregate-only by design. Confirm with the owner that "28 of 38 families opened the result" is acceptable and that per-family "last seen" must stay out of the school UI.
5. **A guardian with children in two schools** must switch workspace first. Assumed acceptable; a cross-workspace "all my children" view is possible but breaks the one-context-per-request model in ARCHITECTURE §3 and is deliberately deferred.
6. **Student accounts.** When the Acadigma Students app arrives (PRODUCT-DECISIONS 1.22), it reads these same views with a student-scoped predicate instead of a guardian-scoped one — which is why the scoping lives in one helper rather than in thirteen queries.
