## a. Who we sell to, in what order

The research carries three different market sizes from one source organisation (K-01). The number of record is **BANBEIS 2022: 137 registered English-medium schools, 71,456 students**. BEMA's "300+ schools / 300,000+ students" is a trade-body claim, labelled as one; GTM's ">1,700 institutions" is an unsourced denominator and is not a planning basis. That segment **cannot support the business** — the research says so and then spends thirteen weeks there.

|            | Segment                                                                                                                                  | Realistic size                                           | When                              | Why                                                                                         |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Tier A** | Private English-medium / **English-version** / semi-English KG–Class 8, 150–700 students, Dhaka + Chattogram, owner reachable in one hop | ~150–350 registered plus an unmeasured semi-English tail | Schools 1–20, Sep 2027 – Jan 2028 | Owner decides alone; no committee cycle; we can physically visit                            |

**"English version" is a third category the ICP never names** — NCTB curriculum taught in English, its own report-card format, growing, sitting exactly where we think we are selling (critic #2). Split it out in every ICP table.

**Competitive reality corrections** (SYNTHESIS K-02 to K-09):

- **Eduman** (Leadswin) claims 5,000+ institutions / 3,000,000+ students / 40+ districts with fees, SMS, results and a live bKash merchant. The largest player on the map is missing from COMPETITORS. Profile it.
- **ClassTune** is the incumbent we actually displace — never profiled, price unknown. The switching cost is 450 re-entered students, 25 re-trained teachers, 700 re-trained guardians and three years of history. Ship an importer for its export.
- **IEIMS / EMIS** are live government systems (Student Unique ID, EIIN logins, no fees) — not a competing ERP, but incumbents' agents file this paperwork free as their stickiest retention service.
- **Bidyaan, Edufy and Sheba Shikkha publish prices**: ৳10–20/student all-in, or ৳2,000–40,000/month bands + one-time + 5% VAT, with Bidyaan bundling a **free biometric device at ৳15/student**. That bundle is our real comparison.
- **Teachmint exited school ERP in April 2026** — remove from live comparables.

**Anti-ICP** keeps on-premise demands, "make it like X for ৳20,000 one-time", universities, and anywhere outside Dhaka/Ctg in the first 90 days. **Deleted: "schools that want fee collection first"** — that rule told our salesperson to refuse the market's anchor module (F-02).

## b. Positioning and the demo

**Not AI.** The incumbent will put "AI-Powered" on his ৳15 tier tomorrow at no premium and be believed for eighteen months, and one garbled Bangla parent message screenshotted into a 261K-member group costs more than his ad budget (SYNTHESIS A-06).

Lead with the three things he says he cannot copy or retrofit (debate 03 §4):

1. **The cover-teacher engine.** 8:05am, a teacher is absent, here is who covers period 3. Forty seconds. Nobody in BD or India has it.
2. **The owner-visible append-only audit trail.** "You can see exactly who changed which mark, and when." Owners are not suspicious of software; they are suspicious of their own office staff.
3. **The 24-hour their-logo প্রগতিপত্র.** His equivalent is a ticket and two weeks. **Only offer it if we can keep it** — deliver it wrong and we are finished.

Attendance stays in the demo — the stopwatch is what he fears most — but the **owner-facing promise becomes** "**by 9:30, every section, every day, and here is the screen that shows me who didn't**" (X-02). Hand the phone to the _youngest_ class teacher, never the oldest in front of her principal (G-15). Supporting claims: no fingerprints on children · one login that stays logged in · every change logged · export any time.

**Teacher seat caps are dead** (P-05): per-seat pricing wearing a hat, which made schools ration logins — suppressing our own north-star metric — and pushed a 450-student school from ৳2,999 to ৳7,999 for five extra logins. Pricing is on students only.

That also resolves the apparent conflict between critic #1 (ceiling ৳4,500–5,000) and critic #4 (the ৳15/student rung): **a 450-student school now sits on Starter at ৳4,900** — 2,200 + 300 × ৳9 — exactly his ceiling. Pro is chosen for features, not headcount.

Against the market ladder: 150 → ৳14.7/student (the ৳15 rung); 300 → ৳16.3; 500 → ৳14.2; 800 → ৳13.0; 1,500 → Enterprise. **Never advertise a per-student rate.** Publish the grid.

**Gross-margin arithmetic, stated once.** Pro at 300 students:
`৳4,900 − ৳870 AI (600 actions × ৳1.45) − ৳350 infra − ৳123 gateway (2.5%) − ৳233 support = **৳3,324 = 67.8% gross margin**.`
The research's ৳3,500 / 1,000-action recommendation yields **42.6%** — a reseller margin, not a SaaS margin (SYNTHESIS A-01, P-04). Budget AI at **cost + 30%** for the next tokenizer change; that scenario takes Pro to 60.6%, survivable here and fatal at ৳3,500.

## d. Fees

- **Offline fee recording moves inside the R1 launch gate.** F-CM-08 P1–6: heads, structures, discounts and approvals, invoice runs, cashier with serial-numbered receipts, arrears ledger, **defaulter list printing as a call sheet with guardian name and phone sorted by amount**. Six parts, no gateway dependency (F-01). Model the real levers too: **block admit card / withhold result if dues > X**, waiver with an approver, sibling discounts.
- **Online payment (P7–14) stays R1.5.** The CFO's objection was to the _online_ rail's unbudgeted cost, not to recording fees; separating them satisfies both critics.
- **School remains merchant of record, always.** Two hostile critics call this the best regulatory decision in the plan. Acadigma takes 0%.
- **Fallback where a merchant account is impossible** — a committee school's licence is in the proprietor's name and its account is joint. Ship a documented **bKash Personal Retail Account path** and **bKash-statement CSV import**, so the clerk's reconciliation stops being a calculator exercise (F-03).
- **bKash merchant (1.85%) is the default rail** for our subscriptions and school fees — days to obtain, no ৳25,500. **aamarPay's education tier (৳4,999 setup, 1.8–2.1% MFS) is the card alternative.** SSLCommerz is deferred to M4. On ৳324,000/month of online fees the MDR spread is ~৳1,944/month **of the school's money** (F-04, F-05). → owner decision.

## e. SMS

- **৳0.75 per UCS-2 segment.** A Bengali fee reminder is ~110 characters = **2 segments**; at ৳0.35 each loses ৳0.37–1.05, i.e. ৳(740)–(2,100) per school per month at 2,000 sends (S-01, S-02).
- **300 segments on Pro, none on Starter, never "unlimited", and always quoted outside the plan price** — a school sending 2,000–2,600/month must not discover its ৳4,900 is really ৳6,400 (S-03).
- **Buy wholesale via a BTRC-enlisted aggregator, sell at ~45–52% margin.** Running SMS at cost is a decision to have no field channel; the incumbent funds his district agents on this spread (S-04).
- **Sender ID registered in the school's name** — parents must never get fee reminders from a mask reading ACADIGMA (S-06).
- **Lead time is a legal-entity dependency, not two engineering parts**: BTRC enlistment and per-operator approval need a company name, trade licence, TIN and NID. **Quote 10 working days from signature, not go-live** (S-05). Mandatory segment-and-taka estimate before every send.

## f. Hiring

- **Not the acquisition engine.** The 3.7M-membership NTRCA cluster waits for a central government recommendation against an MPO vacancy — a school cannot post that job. Strip it out and the addressable audience is ~76K, against Bdjobs (which has candidates) and free Facebook groups (H-01).
- **Ships M4 / R1.5, minimal 4 parts** — postings, public apply, pipeline, scorecards — **sold to the school**, not to teachers as a channel. Resolves D-28 and OQ-16.
- **Candidate browse deleted. Apply-only.** In a city with forty relevant schools whose principals share a WhatsApp group, a browsable availability directory is safe only for the unemployed (H-03).
- **`open_to_work` becomes a private preference that surfaces jobs to the teacher, never the teacher to schools.** If browse ever returns it needs an employer-exclusion array first (H-04).
- **No "Verified" badge** — "Documents checked by Acadigma on {date}", method stated, NTRCA numbers checked against the NTRCA service, rejections never shown to a school and always appealable. Eyeballing a JPEG is the process that passed 1,156 forged certificates (H-05).
- The public apply page must not mint auth accounts from an unauthenticated form — store the application plus a claim token (H-06).

## g. Marketplace

- **The go/no-go gate moves from M6 to immediately after M3, and the default answer is "no."** ~45 parts (≈৳720,000) for **৳8,400/month net** at 1,000 schools — 3–7× beyond the realistic SOM (M-01, M-03). Run only the 9-part pilot slice; no-go leaves a free resource-sharing layer inside F-TE-05.
- **Never pitch seller earnings** until the pilot proves them. At 100 schools the best seller in the country earns ৳1,500/month, against 4–6 hours per pack (M-02).
- **Payout minimum: recommend ৳300, weekly auto-payout**, instant-on-request with the fee stated, bKash cash-out cost shown honestly. Today's design takes up to 29 days to a first payout — the retention event. **D-17 is the owner's; ৳1,000 stands until he says otherwise** (M-05, OQ-15).
- **No seller commissioning.** ৳120,000 of inventory against ৳6,750/yr of take is an 18-year payback — and the incumbent will spend ৳500,000 doing it first and give the results away free (M-04).
- **Watermarking: invisible token only.** A teacher's name on forty handouts in forty households is a personal-safety question, not a DRM detail. On failure retry and queue, never serve untraced (M-06).
- **Workspace switch `marketplace.staff_may_list`, default off**, plus an IP policy on workspace-authored material. Remove the ৳1,000 marketplace credit from Pro's price and every pitch (M-07, P-13).
- **AIT withholding alone justifies the deferral** — challans, certificates and annual returns for hundreds of sellers is a treasury function one person cannot operate (M-08, OQ-2).

## h. AI

- **Allowances are per month, pooled** (D-39): trial 100 lifetime · Starter 200 · Pro 600 · Enterprise contractual. Pooling is also the right _shape_ — teachers' work arrives in Thursday-night bursts, not daily quotas (A-02).
- **Route by language risk, not plan tier.** `min_plan_tier` gates by cost, cost tracks model size, and model size determines Bangla quality — so the rule guarantees the **worst Bengali goes to the schools least able to notice the errors**, contradicting "never ship unreviewed Bangla to parents." Bangla parent-facing output uses the better model on every plan; control cost by limiting _which actions_ a tier gets (A-04, supersedes D-26(4)).
- **No Free tier and no free AI.** See §c.
- **Approval is liability transfer** — the audit row carries the teacher's id, not ours. **No bulk approval on anything a parent will read:** a per-student edit or typed sentence before `parent_visible`, teacher's name on the comment. "Approve all" for forty children at 9pm will be one tap, and the first wrong comment costs the school (A-05).
- **The credit ledger's hard block at zero is non-negotiable.** No bypass, no hand-granted credits without an audit row (A-07).
- `redactForAI()` moves to M0/M1 and **fails closed** — one that fails open is worse than none, because the DPA says it works (A-09).

## i. GTM calendar, channels, loops

**The date nobody wrote down.** R1 needs 149 parts and today is 17 Sep 2026, so **the Sep–Oct 2026 pilot window and the January 2027 closing window are already gone**. Realistic R1 live: ~April 2027. **First real window: Sep 2027 pilots → January 2028 close** (G-01).

**The year in between.** Incorporate within two weeks. Build M0–M3. Recruit **3–5 paid design partners** at ৳2,000/month. Register sender IDs. Run the 24-hour report card offer as a lead magnet — an evening, not a product. Produce Bengali content and the committee pack. Emerge with **two named principals with phone numbers**, the proof #1 will not sign without.

**Pilot in shadow mode, prove in April.** Critics #1 and #4 only appear to conflict. Pilots start **Sep–Oct 2027 alongside the school's existing process** — never as the system of record for the November annual exam, where one wrong GPA means 450 angry guardians. Close at the **January 2028 boundary**; the proof exam is the **first terminal, April–May 2028**. Committee schools sign in January, set up during the Ramadan/Eid closure, go live mid-March (G-02).

**The 36–40 day closure is a first-class product state.** For six weeks a year attendance goes to zero everywhere at once: today's plan fires the churn alert at the entire base, collapses the health score and shows WATA in freefall — while fees are still due and উৎসব ভাতা is the school's biggest cash-out. **Health score, churn thresholds, WATA, attendance % and billing all use `app.is_school_day`** (built at M2 2.2), plus a declared long-holiday mode and a billing answer for the closure month (G-03).

**Founder-capacity-honest funnel.** The month-12 target of 110 schools needs 800+ founder-days against 250 available — **3.2× oversubscribed**, and the ceiling is _onboarding days_, not leads (G-04). So: **make CSV import (F-AC-02 P8) the best screen in the product** — it moves the annual ceiling from ~40 schools to ~150 and is the highest-leverage engineering investment in the plan (G-05); replace on-site training with recorded Bengali videos and a printed one-pager, charging ৳10,000 for optional on-site; target **0.5 founder-days per school**; and plan **18–25 paid schools by Jan 2028**, not 110.

**Channels.** Test the 25% cold-Messenger reply rate on 60 schools before planning on it — the page is run by a student's elder brother who does not read the inbox (G-06). **Promote the guide-book / খাতা / প্রগতিপত্র-booklet seller to a primary channel with a commission structure**: he sees forty principals a month and is trusted by all of them (G-07). Keep two motions — owner-decided Tier A at ~6 weeks, committee Tier B at **10–14 weeks** with a one-page Bengali proposal showing cost-per-student and a three-year total, a মূসক invoice sample and our BIN (G-08).

**PLG loops that survive the safeguarding review:** teacher pull-through card (message only) · waitlists · school public profile (R2) · embeddable রুটিন (R2) · school↔school subscription-month referrals · share-to-WhatsApp **with school-only OG images**.
**Killed:** the unauthenticated single-child parent view, which sends a named child's marks to an unauthenticated URL over channels whose crawlers fetch previews, before any consent exists — it becomes an **invitation** (G-09); OG/share images carrying a child's name, photo, marks or attendance (G-10); marketing QR codes on ID cards and report cards (G-11); the ৳2,000 teacher-to-principal cash bounty (G-12); teacher↔teacher credit referral codes (G-13).

**Outreach ethics, non-negotiable.** No cold DMs to scraped group members, ever; group-admin routes only; no "আপা/ভাই" cold DM from an unknown man to a woman teacher; never ask a teacher to WhatsApp us her original files (G-14); never hand your phone to the oldest teacher in front of her principal (G-15); never put a student's name, marks or photo in any chat — send a permissioned link; never claim "PDPA compliant", "bank-grade security" or "all your data stays in Bangladesh"; never fear-sell with fine amounts.

## j. Compliance and safeguarding, in plain language

1. **No biometric data of children.** QR cards carry a signed random token. We will never add fingerprints; it is not a missing feature.
2. **We keep no NID or birth-certificate _numbers_** — only an attestation (type, last four digits, verifier, date). Scans purge 14 days after the admission decision, with a "re-request this document" flow so schools are not driven back to the almirah.
3. **We cite the Personal Data Protection Act 2026 (Act 63 of 2026)** everywhere; every artefact naming the repealed 2025 Ordinance is corrected first.
4. **We publish where the data lives**, in Bengali — "আপনার তথ্য কোথায় থাকে" — before anyone asks. Today: Mumbai. Minimisation now (~৳25,000); a BD-resident encrypted replica of the sensitive subset (~৳96,000/yr) from month 18. We never say "all your data stays in Bangladesh."
5. **A signed DPA is in the pack before signature**, with our BIN and a Mushak invoice sample.
6. **Consent records carry an honesty field** — `assurance_level` (channel-verified / school-attested / paper / tutor-asserted), in every export and on the owner dashboard. A tick box is not verified consent.
7. **At 18, parental consent lapses automatically**, the school is notified and guardian portal access suspends pending the now-adult student's own decision.
8. **The risk score is disclosable** — out of the parent portal, into every access request with an explanation. The nightly job runs under a role that cannot bypass RLS, so "no automated process touches a human safeguarding flag" is true where claimed.
9. **Removal means removal on the device too** — offline queue, query cache and student-scoped caches purge on the next app open after revocation.
10. **The broadest role cannot see a child's address or photograph by default**; health records and Documents require stepping up.
11. **A staff privacy notice at invitation**, and a written statement that workload, punctuality and eligibility metrics are advisory, never an automated employment decision.
12. **Every change to a child's record is logged permanently and the owner can see it.** Nobody else here sells that.
