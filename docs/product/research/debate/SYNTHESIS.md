# Adversarial review — synthesis and adjudication

**Date:** 2026-09-17 · **Adjudicator:** research synthesis (Opus) · **Status:** final before build

Five researchers wrote `research/*.md`. Five critics attacked them from opposing chairs:
**#1** school owner (Bangla-medium, 450 students) · **#2** teacher (English-version, Chattogram) ·
**#3** incumbent's head of product · **#4** fractional CFO / seed investor · **#5** data-protection lawyer + safeguarding adviser.

This document rules on every contested claim. The surviving strategy is in
`research/MARKET-STRATEGY.md`; the executable change list is in `research/DECISION-CHANGES.md`.

**Verdict vocabulary.** **UPHELD** — the original claim stands. **REVISED** — the critic is right in
part; the corrected form is stated. **REJECTED** — the original claim is withdrawn. **OWNER** — the
call belongs to Mahadi, not the lead; the recommendation is stated and the default named.

**Evidence strength.** _measured_ — a number we or a critic computed from a primary rate card,
statute, review corpus or vendor page. _sourced_ — a cited third party asserts it, unrecomputed.
_inferred_ — reasoning from pattern, persona or analogy; no direct evidence.

**Where critics disagree with each other**, the row says so and rules. The four substantive
critic-vs-critic conflicts are rows P-04 (owner wants ৳4,500 / CFO wants ৳4,900+), F-01 (owner wants
fees in R1 / CFO says the entity and merchant costs are unbudgeted), A-03 (teacher wants a free tier
that beats ChatGPT / CFO wants Free killed), and G-02 (owner says Sep–Dec is the worst pilot window /
CFO says Sep 2027 is the only window left).
