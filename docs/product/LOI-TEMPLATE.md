- **Pilot price:** ৳2,000/month for 8 to 10 weeks, covering one full terminal exam.
- **Success criteria (agreed before the pilot starts):** (1) attendance recorded on at least 80% of school days; (2) at least one report card or mark sheet generated in the system; (3) the class teacher confirms it is faster than the paper register.
- **Conversion:** if the criteria above are met, the pilot converts automatically to the chosen plan in Section 2 at the price shown — no percentage discount beyond the pilot price already given.
- **Price lock:** the price in Section 2 is locked for **24 months** from the conversion date, after which a maximum 5% annual increase may apply, announced at least 60 days in advance.
- **Founding recognition:** with the school's written permission, the school's name may be listed as a founding school on Acadigma's public website and in case studies. This is optional and can be declined without affecting the pilot.
