# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: {{date}} · Version {{n}}**

## 1. In short

Acadigma Campus is school software. Schools use it to keep attendance, marks, timetables and school records, and to let parents see their own child's progress.

- **Your child's school decides** what information is kept about your child and for how long. The school is legally responsible for it. We look after the software and follow the school's instructions.
- **We do not sell your information.** We do not show advertisements. We do not use your child's information to train artificial-intelligence models.
- **We do not collect fingerprints, face scans or any other biometric information from children.**
- **Children do not have accounts** in Acadigma Campus. Only adults — school staff and parents — sign in.
- Your information is stored on secure servers in **Mumbai, India**, operated by our hosting provider. We explain why, and what that means, in §7.
- You can ask to see, correct or delete information. Some school records — attendance and exam results — must be kept by law, and we explain that honestly in §8.

If you only read one section, read §8 (your rights) and §7 (where your data is).

## 2. Who we are

Acadigma is {{legal entity name and registration number}}, {{registered address, Bangladesh}}.

Our **Chief Data Officer** is {{name}}. You can reach them at **privacy@acadigma.com**. If you think information has been exposed or is at risk, write to **security@acadigma.com**.

Acadigma Campus is part of the Acadigma suite. This policy covers Acadigma Campus, our website, and the Android and Windows versions of the app.

## 3. Two different situations — please read this part

### 3.1 When your school uses Acadigma (most of what is in the app)

If you are a parent, a student's family, a teacher or a school staff member, then for almost everything in the app **your school is in charge**. In the language of the law, your school is the **data fiduciary** (also called the controller) and Acadigma is the **data processor**.

In practice this means:

- Your school decides what to record about a student, who at the school can see it, and how long to keep it.
- We only do what the school asks us to do. We have a written agreement with every school that says so.
- If you want something changed or deleted, **ask the school first**. If you ask us, we will pass it to the school within 3 working days and tell you we have done so.
- Your school may also have its own privacy notice. It applies alongside this one.

### 3.2 When Acadigma is in charge

For some things we decide, and we are responsible:

- your **account** — your name, email, phone, password, the devices you sign in from;
- your **personal workspace** if you are a teacher or tutor — your own files, diary, students you tutor privately, your CV and job applications;
- the **marketplace** — seller profiles, identity checks, payments and payouts;
- security records — logs of who did what, so we can investigate problems.

For these, you deal with us directly.

## 4. What we collect

### 4.1 About students (kept for the school)

- **Basic details:** name, date of birth, gender, photo, student ID, class and section, admission and enrolment history.
- **Family details:** guardians' names, relationship, phone numbers, email addresses, and sometimes occupation and address.
- **School records:** attendance, exam marks, grades and GPA, report cards, homework and submissions, behaviour notes.
- **Sensitive details, only if the school collects them and you agree:** health conditions, allergies, medications, blood group, and medical documents such as prescriptions or immunisation cards; religion.
- **Identity documents, for a short time:** a national ID or birth-certificate scan, if the school needs it to confirm who a child is at admission. **We delete these {{90 days}} after admission is confirmed and keep only a note that the check was done.** [VERIFY: this is true once the auto-purge job ships — see COMPLIANCE-PDPA §9 item P27. Do not publish this sentence before then.]

Religion and health information are **optional**. A school does not have to collect them, and where they are collected only the school's owner, administrators, the class teacher and designated first-aid staff can see health information.

### 4.2 About parents and guardians

Name, relationship to the child, phone, email, and — once you accept an invitation — your account details, the devices you use, and the messages and announcements the school sends you.

### 4.3 About school staff and teachers

Name and contact details, role and title, qualifications, joining date, attendance and leave, cover-teaching assignments, and — visible only to the school's owner and administrators — pay rate information.

### 4.4 About job applicants

Your application, the stage it has reached, interview notes and scorecards, and any documents you choose to share (CV, certificates, and sometimes identity documents). **You control your documents.** A school only sees them while you have approved a request, and that approval lasts **30 days** unless you renew it. You can withdraw it at any moment.

### 4.5 About sellers on the marketplace

Your seller profile, the identity documents and photograph you provide so we can confirm who you are before we send you money, your payout account details (stored encrypted and shown only in masked form), and your sales, earnings and payouts.

Only named Acadigma staff can see identity documents. **Schools cannot see them. Buyers cannot see them. Other sellers cannot see them.** Sellers must be 18 or over.

### 4.6 About everyone who uses the app

Your account details, your preferences (language, theme, notifications), the devices and browsers you sign in from, and records of your activity in the app — what was changed, by whom and when — which we keep so schools can answer questions about their own records and so we can investigate security problems.

We store a **one-way scrambled version of your IP address**, not the address itself.

### 4.7 What we never collect

- **Fingerprints, face scans, iris scans or any other biometric information** — from anyone, and especially not from children. The QR code on a student ID card is a random code, not a body measurement.
- **Card numbers.** Payments go through SSLCommerz's own secure page; card details never reach our systems.
- **Your location.** We do not track where you or your child are.
- **Advertising or tracking information.** There are no advertising tools in the app.

## 6. Artificial intelligence — exactly what we send

Teachers can ask the app to help write a lesson plan, a worksheet, a quiz, a notice or a message to parents. To do that, the text of the request is sent to **Anthropic**, an AI company in the **United States**, which returns the result.

**What we remove before anything is sent:**

- students' family names and full names — at most a **first name**, and only where the result needs to address the child;
- dates of birth and ages — we send the **class level** instead;
- ID numbers of any kind, phone numbers, email addresses and addresses;
- **health conditions, allergies and medications** — these are never sent;
- **religion** — never sent;
- individual marks attached to a named child;
- guardians' names and contact details.

**We never send documents.** No medical file, identity document, CV or identity-check document is ever sent to the AI service. The only document type that goes is a **syllabus** — a curriculum document with no personal information in it.

**Anthropic does not use this content to train its models** [VERIFY: confirm in writing against the current commercial terms before publishing, and confirm the retention window — COMPLIANCE-PDPA §5.2].

When a teacher types a request themselves, the app reminds them not to include names, health details or ID numbers, and our system removes them before sending.

**We do not make decisions about your child automatically.** The app shows teachers an indicator based on attendance, marks and behaviour to help them notice a student who may need attention. It is a simple, published calculation — never a secret AI judgement — the reasons are always shown, and a teacher decides what to do. A teacher's own note is never removed by the system.

## 7. Where your information is kept

- **Our database and files** — everything described in §4 — are stored with **Supabase**, on Amazon Web Services infrastructure in **Mumbai, India (region ap-south-1)**.
- **Our application servers** run in the same Mumbai region.
- **Our website and app are delivered** through Vercel's worldwide network, which speeds up loading by serving pages from a nearby location. That network handles your request as it passes through; it does not store your school's records.
- **AI requests** go to Anthropic in the **United States**, with the strict limits in §6.
- **Emails** are sent through Resend. **Error reports** go to Sentry, and they contain identifiers only, never a student's details.
- **Payments** are handled by **SSLCommerz, in Bangladesh**.

We keep this list up to date at {{/legal/subprocessors}} and give schools **30 days' notice** before we add or change a provider.

**Why this matters, honestly.** Bangladesh's data-protection law limits sending certain kinds of personal information abroad, and the rules on which information and which countries are still being settled by the government. We have chosen the nearest major region to Bangladesh, we keep as little sensitive information as possible, we delete identity documents quickly, and we are planning for a Bangladesh-based option. If the rules require us to keep data inside Bangladesh, we will move it and tell schools before we do. **We do not claim that your data is stored in Bangladesh, because today it is not.**

## 8. Your rights

You can ask to:

1. **See** what is held about you or your child, and get a copy;
2. **Correct** anything that is wrong;
3. **Delete** information that is no longer needed;
4. **Receive a copy in a portable format** — we provide JSON and CSV files in a ZIP, with a plain explanation of each file. If the government prescribes a particular format, we will use that instead;
5. **Withdraw consent** you gave earlier;
6. **Object** to something we or the school are doing;
7. **Complain** — to the school, to us, or to the national data-protection authority.

**How to ask.** In the app: _Settings → Your data_. A parent can find it at _Family → Settings → Your data_. Or email **privacy@acadigma.com**. A school can also make a request for you.

**How long we take.** We acknowledge within **3 working days** and finish within **20 working days**. Corrections within **30 days**. If a request is unusually complex we may take another 20 working days and will tell you why.

**Proving who you are.** We will check you are who you say you are before we hand over information about a child. This protects your child.

## 9. Who can see what

- **Teachers** see the students in their own classes and subjects. Health information only where they need it.
- **School administrators and the owner** see their own school, and can see a log of every change made to any record.
- **Parents** see **only their own children** — and only attendance, marks and report cards, timetable, exam schedule, homework, behaviour notes the school has marked visible to parents, and school announcements.
- **Other schools cannot see your school.** Every record is locked to one school by the database itself, not just by the screen you see. We test this automatically on every single change we make to the software.
- **Acadigma staff** do not browse school records. A small number of named staff can see marketplace identity checks. For support, a school owner can grant one of our staff time-limited access, and that access is recorded. We never sign in as you.
- **Private messages between two people are private.** School administrators cannot read them.

## 10. How we protect information

- Every record is protected by rules inside the database itself, checked again by our servers, and tested automatically before any change is released.
- Medical documents, identity documents and other private files are never available at a public link. Each download uses a **one-time link that expires in 5 minutes**, and every download is recorded.
- Payout account details are stored **encrypted**.
- Information travels over an encrypted connection and is encrypted where it is stored.
- Every change to a record is written to a permanent log that no one — including us — can edit or delete.
- Passwords are never stored in a readable form, and we check new passwords against lists of common ones.
- We run automated security checks on every code change and have the system tested by independent security professionals before launch.

No system is perfectly secure, and we will not pretend otherwise. What we can promise is that we build with the assumption that we will be attacked, and that we will tell you quickly if something goes wrong.

## 11. If something goes wrong

If information is exposed, we will tell the affected school **within 72 hours** of confirming it, with what happened, what information was involved, how many people are affected, and what we are doing. Your school will then tell you, and will notify the authority if required. Where the information is ours — accounts, marketplace, identity checks — we will tell you directly.

## 13. Children

Acadigma Campus holds information about children because schools keep records about children. We take that seriously.

- **Children under 18 do not have accounts.** Only adults sign in.
- A school may only keep a child's information with the agreement of that child's **parent or legal guardian**. We provide the tools for schools to obtain and record that agreement, and we keep a record of who agreed, when, in which language, and to exactly which words.
- **We never show advertisements to anyone**, and we never build advertising or behavioural profiles of children.
- We do not make automatic decisions about children. See §6.
- A parent can withdraw agreement at any time by telling the school. The school will explain which records it must still keep.

## 14. Changes to this policy

We will publish a new version here with the date and a short summary of what changed.

- If the change is **significant**, we will email school owners at least **30 days** in advance, and school owners will be asked to accept the new version when they next sign in.
- If a change affects a consent you gave, we will ask you again, in plain language, explaining what changed. **We will not cut off your access to your child's information while you decide.**
- Old versions stay available so you can see what you agreed to.
