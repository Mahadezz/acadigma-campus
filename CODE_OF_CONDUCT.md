# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Acadigma Campus to be a project where anyone can contribute and be treated decently. Everyone involved — contributors, reviewers, reporters, users — commits to making participation free of harassment, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that builds this project:

- Being direct about code and kind about people. Review the change, not the person who wrote it.
- Saying what you actually checked. "LGTM" on a PR that touches access control is not a review.
- Giving and accepting feedback on technical grounds, and changing your mind when the evidence says so.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes, fixing them, and writing the test so they do not recur.
- Explaining context to people who do not have it yet, rather than assuming they should.

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information — physical or email address, phone number — without explicit permission.
- Dismissing or belittling someone's question, experience level, or English proficiency.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Handling data is part of conduct

This project holds children's records, medical information and identity documents. Two things follow, and they are not negotiable:

- **Never use real user data for testing, demos, screenshots or debugging.** The seed generates synthetic data; use it. A screenshot in a PR containing a real name is a conduct issue as well as a privacy one.
- **If you discover a vulnerability or an exposure, report it privately** through [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md). Do not demonstrate it publicly, do not explore further than needed to confirm it, and do not retain anything you find.

## Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any chat or call about the project — and when representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the project owner at `conduct@acadigma.app`. All reports will be reviewed promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy and safety will be respected. The owner is obligated to keep reports confidential.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code in good faith may face consequences determined by the project's leadership.

### Enforcement guidelines

**1. Correction** — Inappropriate or unprofessional language or behaviour.
_Consequence:_ a private written warning explaining what was wrong and why, and a request for a public apology where appropriate.

**2. Warning** — A single incident or a series of actions.
_Consequence:_ a warning with consequences for continued behaviour. No interaction with the people involved, including unsolicited interaction with those enforcing this Code, for a specified period. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

**3. Temporary ban** — A serious violation, including sustained inappropriate behaviour.
_Consequence:_ a temporary ban from any interaction or public communication with the project for a specified period. No public or private interaction with the people involved during this period. Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

**4. Permanent ban** — A pattern of violation, sustained inappropriate behaviour, harassment of an individual, or aggression toward or disparagement of classes of individuals. Also: deliberate misuse of real user data, or publicly disclosing a vulnerability instead of reporting it privately.
_Consequence:_ a permanent ban from any public interaction within the project.
